Terms: sharing and adaptation are allowed, including commercial use, if appropriate credit is given, a link to the licence is supplied and changes are indicated. No endorsement may be implied.

Why it can fit: it permits reuse and modification with attribution. It is a content licence, not a software licence; the specific item must actually be offered under CC BY 4.0 by its rights holder.

Use here: no third-party CC BY 4.0 dataset is currently imported. BookRank states that its scores, ranks and confidence values are CC BY 4.0, but the Vault previously imported additional fields whose licence scope was not sufficiently clear. The import and displayed scores have been disabled rather than assuming that the licence covers every field.

IMDb

Work cards may open an IMDb search. The Vault does not scrape IMDb, call a data endpoint, store IMDb ratings, reproduce reviews or copy images. IMDb's limited non-commercial dataset permission has strict conditions and prohibits republishing the data to create a movie database. This project does not rely on that permission.

Why the link is retained: it directs visitors to the original service to check information there; it does not reproduce IMDb content in the Vault. The link is not a claim of endorsement or partnership.

External title lookup via GoogleNo RT data import

Rotten Tomatoes

Each catalogue work now has a “Find on Rotten Tomatoes” link. To respect Rotten Tomatoes' published terms, this link opens a Google search restricted to Rotten Tomatoes pages; it does not deep-link directly to an RT title or search page. Visitors can choose an appropriate Rotten Tomatoes result themselves.

Important terms boundary: Rotten Tomatoes' Terms of Use, last updated 6 January 2026, state that users may not link to pages other than the homepage (deep linking) without Fandango's prior written consent. The Vault therefore does not generate direct per-title Rotten Tomatoes links unless written permission is obtained.

The Vault does not scrape Rotten Tomatoes, call its data endpoints, import Tomatometer or Popcornmeter values, copy reviews, or use posters and stills. The search link sends the visitor to Google; the visitor decides whether to open a Rotten Tomatoes result. No RT content is copied into the Vault.

Open Library

Book cards may open an Open Library search. The Vault does not query its API in bulk, download covers, copy ratings or use it as a third-party database backend. Open Library asks applications to make useful human-facing requests and avoid bulk harvesting; it offers monthly data dumps for bulk access.

Why the link is retained: a visitor chooses to open the external search. The Vault does not transfer or republish Open Library records.

CATALOGUE CONTENT

What appears in the Vault

Titles and factual metadata

The catalogue is a manually curated selection of work titles and basic factual fields such as creator, release year, media type and genre. The project does not copy plot summaries, editorial reviews, biographies, database descriptions or user-generated text. Facts are not automatically free of all legal restrictions: database rights, source terms and the way a collection was obtained still matter. Where provenance or permission cannot be established, the field is omitted rather than copied.

Posters, covers, stills and logos

No third-party film posters, television stills, book covers, album artwork, game artwork, service logos or promotional images are included. Only original local interface artwork created for this project is used. A public image search result or an image visible on a website is not permission to reuse it.

Ratings and reviews

The Vault does not import IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, BookRank, Google Books, Hardcover or other third-party numeric scores or review text. The owner's rating remains “Not rated yet” until the maintainer enters an original rating. Visitor ratings and work suggestions are stored locally in that visitor's browser and are not transmitted to the maintainer or published as shared community data. The forms are demonstration-only until a backend, privacy notice, moderation process and data-retention policy are implemented.

Names and trademarks

Titles, creator names and service names are used only as identifiers or plain-text references where needed to describe the catalogue and link to the original service. This does not grant trademark rights or imply sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement. Third-party logos and brand artwork are not reproduced.

REUSE CHECKLIST

Rules before adding external material

  • Identify the original creator, rights holder and exact source URL.
  • Verify the licence for the specific file, dataset or API field. Do not infer permission from public availability or a search result.
  • Check whether commercial use, modification, redistribution, caching and inclusion in an open-source project are permitted.
  • Meet every condition: attribution, licence link, change notices, copyright notices, ShareAlike terms and source-specific limits.
  • Do not mix incompatible licences or assume the project's MIT licence overrides third-party terms.
  • If the licence is missing, ambiguous, incompatible or impossible to comply with, do not import the material. Remove it and disable the related feature until permission is verified.
  • Keep a record of the source, version, retrieval date, licence and required attribution for every imported dataset or asset.

Current implementation decision: the BookRank data importer and score display have been disabled. The previous importer consumed book titles, authors, years, rating counts and other fields alongside scores. BookRank's published CC BY 4.0 statement specifically identifies scores, ranks and confidence values; its identifier crosswalk is CC0. Because the licence statement did not clearly establish that every field consumed by the Vault was covered for this exact reuse, the conservative choice is not to import any BookRank records. The app clears the old BookRank browser cache when the updated version runs.

This is a practical project policy, not legal advice or a guarantee. Licence and service terms can change. Re-check original terms before adding or re-enabling any source.
